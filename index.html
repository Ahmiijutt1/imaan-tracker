Privacy

The PIN hides the app on this device and locks it after a minute away. It is not encryption, so use your device lock too. If you forget the PIN, restore a backup made before you set it.

About and sources

  • Prayer times are calculated on your device with standard astronomical methods (Karachi, Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian and others). Compare them with your local mosque.
  • Hijri dates use the Umm al-Qura calendar from your browser. Moon sighting where you live can differ by a day, so use the adjustment in the Calendar tab.
  • Duas and adhkar show their sources. Some repeat counts are common practice, and scholars differ on how well they are authenticated.
  • Excused days, fasting and make-up prayers follow widely accepted guidance, but every situation is different.

Please check anything you are unsure about with a scholar you trust. This is a personal tracker, not a source of religious rulings.
